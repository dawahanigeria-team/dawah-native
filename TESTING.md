# App Testing Feedback

I found a few things that are not working as expected.

### 1. Clicking on a featured lecture plays it instead of opening it.

When I tap on one of the big slides on the home screen, it just starts playing the audio. I expected it to take me to the lecture's detail page.

### 2. Tapping on a regular lecture card also plays it directly.

Same as above, but for the smaller lecture cards in the lists like "Recently Played". Tapping them should open the lecture page, not start playing the audio right away.

### 3. Can't cancel or resume downloads.

If you start downloading a lecture, there's no way to stop it. And if the download gets interrupted, you can't resume it from where it left off.
Also the audio stops playing whlie the screen is locked.
whlie the call comes in the audio was still playning till the calll has been picked the it stopped.


I noticed that there's no warning if you try to download a lecture without enough space on your phone. It will probably just crash or show a weird error.

### 4. The loading indicators could be better.

When the app is loading content, it just shows a spinning wheel. On a slow connection, this can make the screen jump around. can we  use those placeholder "skeleton" loaders that look like the content that's about to appear.

### 5. The app doesn't ask for permission to save files.

When I tried to download a lecture, the app didn't ask me for permission to access my phone's storage. If I had denied permission, the app would have probably crashed.
